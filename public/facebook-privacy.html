Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

1. Introduction

Post Meeting Content Generator ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and profile information from your Google account
  • Calendar Data: Meeting information, attendees, and meeting times from your Google Calendar
  • Meeting Transcripts: Transcripts of your meetings processed through Recall.ai
  • Social Media Tokens: Access tokens for LinkedIn and Facebook to enable posting functionality
  • User Preferences: Your automation settings and bot configuration preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Sync and display your calendar events
  • Schedule and manage meeting notetakers via Recall.ai
  • Generate AI-powered social media posts and follow-up emails from meeting transcripts
  • Post content to your connected social media accounts (LinkedIn, Facebook) as requested
  • Store your preferences and automation settings

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Recall.ai: To provide meeting transcription services
  • Google: To access your calendar data (with your explicit permission)
  • LinkedIn/Facebook: To post content on your behalf (with your explicit permission)
  • AI Services: Google Gemini API for generating posts and emails (transcripts are sent for processing)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your social media accounts at any time
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us or use the data deletion feature in Settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app settings or your account dashboard.
