Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: December 2024

Your Right to Delete Data

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from Post Meeting Content Generator at any time. This page explains how to do so.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete Through the App (Recommended)

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to the Settings page
  3. Scroll to the "Account" section
  4. Click "Delete All My Data" button
  5. Confirm the deletion

This will permanently delete all your data from our system, including:

  • Your user account
  • All meeting records and transcripts
  • All social media connections and tokens
  • All automation settings
  • All calendar sync data

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access your account, you can request data deletion by sending an email from the email address associated with your account with "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your data, we will remove:

  • Account Information: Email, name, profile data
  • Calendar Data: All synced calendar events and meeting records
  • Meeting Transcripts: All stored transcripts from Recall.ai
  • Social Media Tokens: All access tokens for LinkedIn and Facebook
  • Generated Content: All AI-generated posts and emails
  • Settings: All user preferences and automation configurations

Third-Party Data

Please note that deleting your data from our Service does not automatically delete data stored with third-party services:

  • Google: Your calendar data remains in your Google account
  • LinkedIn/Facebook: Posts already published remain on those platforms
  • Recall.ai: You may need to contact them separately to delete transcripts

You will need to manage deletion of data from these services separately through their respective platforms.

Deletion Timeline

Data deletion requests are typically processed within:

  • Immediate: When deleted through the app, data is removed immediately
  • Email Requests: Processed within 30 days of receipt
  • Backups: Backup copies are deleted within 90 days

Important Note

Warning: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted, you will need to create a new account and reconnect all services if you wish to use the Service again.

Contact Us

If you have questions about data deletion or need assistance, please contact us through the app settings or your account dashboard.
